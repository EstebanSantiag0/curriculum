# Chase Swiss fellowships first, big tech second

Esteban's most realistic summer-2027 applications are four Swiss funded research programmes, not corporate internships: EPFL E3 (due Sunday 1 November), Summer@EPFL (29 November), the ETH Zurich Summer Research Fellowship (1 November to 16 December) and CERN's openlab and Summer Student programmes (calls open in November). All four admit students enrolled at any university, pay CHF 1,800 to 4,000 a month, and handle the Swiss permit themselves, which is the obstacle that shuts nearly every corporate door for a Mexican student ([ETH](https://inf.ethz.ch/studies/summer-research-fellowship/how-to-apply.html); [EPFL E3](https://actu.epfl.ch/news/apply-to-the-e3-epfl-excellence-in-engineering-p-6/)). NVIDIA is reachable but unresolved: it has bachelor-friendly postings in the US (several close Tuesday 3 November), China (about 20 of 86 roles), Munich, Warsaw and Toronto, yet states no visa policy anywhere, and all seven of its Zurich intern roles demand an MSc or PhD ([NVIDIA careers](https://nvidia.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com/NVIDIAExternalCareerSite)). Oracle's US internships bar sponsorship and require US residence or a US university, so the only credible Oracle route is an email application to Oracle Labs Zurich, where the permit question is unanswered ([Oracle Labs GraalVM posting](https://www.ifi.uzh.ch/dam/jcr:55035e44-901c-4af5-96a6-3e3d92b34773/GraalVM_DB_AI_Research_Internship_2026.pdf)). Sweden is moderate (a dedicated paid-traineeship permit, but an employer must sponsor and decisions take up to four months), Ireland is low to moderate, and China is a long shot except research visits such as Tsinghua's Global Research Program. In Mexico, BMW San Luis Potosí, GM, Amazon, Intel and NXP take students at his stage, but they are 6- to 12-month on-site stints that collide with the UTM calendar. The Ford claim is half right and mislabeled: Ford de México runs no tuition scholarship tied to an internship; it runs **Ford Champs**, a paid (about MXN 8,000 a month, 2025 figure), roughly 12-month, 25-hour-a-week programme for students in their **last two semesters**, so Esteban's first window is July or October 2028, or March 2029 ([Ford](https://www.fromtheroad.ford.com/mx/es/articles/2026/julio/ford-fortalece-el-desarrollo-de-talento-en-mexico-con-una-ruta-q)).

## Switzerland's funded fellowships are the only door that opens

The ETH Zurich Student Summer Research Fellowship is the strongest fit. It is open to "all students worldwide" except ETH's own, runs 2 July to 27 August 2027, pays **CHF 4,000** to cover housing and living, covers travel and visa costs, and decides by the end of February; Computer Systems is one of its nine research areas, which suits a hand-built RV32I core ([ETH SSRF](https://inf.ethz.ch/studies/summer-research-fellowship.html); [how to apply](https://inf.ethz.ch/studies/summer-research-fellowship/how-to-apply.html)). Eligibility is two years of study completed by the start and graduation in the year after the fellowship or later, both of which he meets; the application needs an English CV, the official transcript, a one-page motivation letter and one or two preferred research areas. Programme-arranged housing is deducted from the stipend, and visa responsibility formally sits with the fellow though ETH helps and reimburses ([ETH FAQ](https://inf.ethz.ch/studies/summer-research-fellowship/faqs.html)). The risk is calendar: fellows must be present all of July and August, and the notes' timeline starts UTM's semester 7 in August 2027.

EPFL offers two cheaper but more flexible doors. E3 takes bachelor's and master's students from other institutions who have completed two bachelor years, from any nationality, for 8 to 12 weeks starting between 1 May and October; EPFL sends visa instructions but the student pays embassy fees, and it reserves the right to cancel for geopolitical reasons ([E3 FAQ](https://eee.epfl.ch/faq/)). Summer@EPFL, run by the School of Computer and Communication Sciences, covers computer engineering from the second bachelor year, pays CHF 1,800 a month and offers projects "competitively to students with excellent academic results", which his 9.0 GPA supports ([Summer@EPFL](https://summer.epfl.ch/)). The EPFL Excellence Research Internship Program is closed to him: it requires nomination from a named partner (McGill, UBC, Toronto, Waterloo, NTU, Carnegie Mellon, Georgia Tech, Johns Hopkins, UCSD, UIUC), and UTM is not among them ([ERIP](https://www.epfl.ch/education/international/en/coming-to-epfl/research-internships/eligibility-and-application)).

CERN is the most selective and the most uncertain. The Summer Student Programme and the computing-focused openlab programme both require six semesters (three years) of study completed by the start, nine weeks for openlab starting 21 or 28 June 2027, and CHF 94 per day net ([CERN Summer Studentship](https://careers.cern/summer); [openlab](https://openlab.cern/cern-openlab-summer-student-programme)). Two details matter for Esteban. Mexico is neither a Member State nor an Associate Member, and the non-Member State track is described in a 2019 vacancy as limited to eight weeks, wording a 2026 listing reportedly repeats ([CERN member states](http://home.cern/about/who-we-are/our-stakeholders); [2019 vacancy](https://jobs.smartrecruiters.com/CERN/743999679917538-summer-student-programme-member-and-non-member-states-)). And selection is steep: 60 non-Member State students were supported among 341 summer students in 2025 ([CERN & Society Foundation](https://cernandsocietyfoundation.cern/non-member-state-summer-student-programme/)), while the 2026 cohort was chosen from more than 21,000 applicants ([CERN EP news](https://ep-news.web.cern.ch/one-summer-a-world-of-possibilities-cerns-summer-student-programme-2026/)), a ratio near 2 percent across different years. **Inferred risk:** semester 6 at UTM ends around June 2027, so "three years completed by June" is borderline until UTM certifies it.

Corporate Switzerland is closed for structural reasons that no CV improvement fixes. Canton Zurich allows a foreign student to do an internship only if it is **mandatory** for the degree, once, for up to 12 months in total, at firms where short-term foreign staff stay under 25 percent of the workforce, and with a university letter confirming both enrolment and the requirement, typically through placement organisations such as IAESTE or AIESEC ([Canton Zurich](https://www.zh.ch/en/wirtschaft-arbeit/erwerbstaetigkeit-auslaender/studierende-und-praktikanten.html)). The federal trainee agreements cover 15 countries, not Mexico, and require a completed degree ([SEM](https://www.sem.admin.ch/sem/de/home/themen/arbeit/berufspraktikum.html)). Google Zurich's 2027 intern posting required enrolment at an EMEA school, offered no sponsorship and closed on 23 October ([Google posting copy](https://jobs.anitab.org/companies/google-24698/jobs/92038493-software-engineering-site-reliability-engineering-bs-ms-intern-2027)), and IBM Research Zurich's recent postings mostly ask for master's enrolment ([IBM Research Zurich](https://www.jobup.ch/en/jobs/detail/af49676f-1329-41cf-9c46-260000691d1c), snippet only). **Inferred:** a Swiss corporate internship becomes feasible only if UTM certifies a mandatory practicum and IAESTE or the employer files the permit, which points to the later *prácticas profesionales* stage, not summer 2027.

Ireland is harder. Across Switzerland, Sweden and Ireland, the permit regimes each name the same intermediary, which makes **IAESTE** the highest-leverage single contact. IAESTE's official list shows "Mexico, ITD", a May 2026 article puts the national office at the Instituto Tecnológico de Durango open to students from any Mexican university, and IAESTE reports paid placements such as CHF 2,500 a month in Switzerland and CNY 3,000 in China ([IAESTE students](https://iaeste.org/students); [IAESTE internships](https://iaeste.org/internships)). A Peruvian committee lists Oracle Labs among collaborating employers, which suggests the Labs-via-IAESTE path exists, though nothing confirms Mexican access ([UDEP IAESTE FAQ](https://www.udep.edu.pe/internacional/wp-content/uploads/sites/27/2026/08/IAESTE-Preguntas-frecuentes-Word.pdf)).

## Conclusion

The research reorders the question. What separates a workable programme from a dead end is not the employer's prestige but whether a third party carries the immigration burden: ETH, EPFL and CERN do it by design, Sweden and Ireland by employer-filed permits, IAESTE through its reciprocal exchange network, and Mexican employers because no permit is needed. NVIDIA's and Oracle's own postings carry no such mechanism, so for them the work is not applying but obtaining a written answer about J-1 or permit sponsorship from a recruiter or from the Oracle Labs address. That answer, plus one meeting with UTM on calendar, leave and a certified mandatory practicum, unlocks more options than any CV edit.

The second implication is timing. Summer 2027 is a short window, roughly July by the notes' semester timeline (**Inferred**, pending UTM's calendar), for a student whose semesters run into August, which favours 8- to 12-week fellowships over 6- to 12-month corporate stints, and it is the cheapest moment to build the credentials that NVIDIA China, Oracle Labs, J-1 hosts and Ford Champs will reward in 2028. A Swiss fellowship, a SystemVerilog port of the RISC-V core with a real testbench, and a Java or compiler project for the GraalVM team would make his summer-2028 applications stronger than anything he can submit this week, while the Ford route needs only patience until July 2028 and a conversation with Ford de México about whether "last semesters" means two or four.
