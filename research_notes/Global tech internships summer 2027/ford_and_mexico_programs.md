## Q1. Does Ford (Mexico) offer scholarships or funded internships for university students? What are the real program names, eligibility, pay, timing?

### Takeaway
The claim is half-right but mislabeled. Ford de México does NOT run a tuition "scholarship that funds an internship"; what exists is **Ford Champs**, a paid (stipend), roughly one-year, part-time student program for university students in their **last two semesters**, with ~120 places a year and ~90% converting to full-time, plus **Ford Academies** (PD / IT / Supply Chain) for recent graduates. In Mexican usage that is a "beca/becario" experience, which is probably what Esteban heard about. Separately, Ford Philanthropy and the Ford/Lincoln dealers' civic committee fund merit scholarships (US$3.4M cumulative since 2017) tied to the Escuelas Ford network, which is not an internship program. Esteban is not eligible for Ford Champs until about Aug 2028 (his last two semesters).

## Q2. Do Ford's global programs (Dearborn, Germany, Turkey, India, China) or Ford Fund/Philanthropy scholarships abroad fund internships open to Mexican students?

### Takeaway
No. The Ford global programs found are not accessible to a Mexican undergraduate studying in Mexico: the US Summer Internship requires US work authorization, Ford Fund's university scholarships found are US-focused or run by individual UK/Australian universities, and no foreign-student route was found for Germany or Turkey.

### Cited Findings
- Ford US "Summer Intern Program": 10-12 week paid internships in engineering, software development, design and business; "Program structure and availability vary by region." [P] — [Ford Careers - Students & Graduates](https://www.careers.ford.com/students-graduates)
- Ford Mexico is not described as a destination for the US program on that page; Mexico appears only in the location filters. [P] — same page.
- Ford postings state candidates "must be legally authorized to work in the United States" (verified at hire); a 2027-28 third-party guide says F-1 CPT is handled case by case. Typical 2027 posting wording: graduate and be available for full-time work before Sept 2027 (Sept 2028 for sophomores). [A] — [IIBA job board posting](https://careercenter.iiba.org/job/2026-product-development-engineer-summer-internship/82140018); [Forage guide](https://www.theforage.com/blog/companies/ford-internships) (exact page carrying the quote not confirmed)
- Ford College Graduate (FCG) is a US rotational program for new graduates; no Mexico-based FCG found. [P] — [Ford Careers - Students & Graduates](https://www.careers.ford.com/students-graduates)
- Ford Otosan (Turkey) has a summer-term internship application page; nothing found on eligibility for foreign students. [P] — [Ford Otosan internship page](https://fordotosan.com.tr/en/careers/career-opportunities/apply-for-an-internship)
- Ford Germany (Cologne) internship postings exist (marketing/sales, etc.); no official statement on non-EU students found. [P/A] — [Ford.de careers](https://ford.de/ford-entdecken/careers)
- Ford Fund/Ford Philanthropy university scholarships found: Ford Auto Tech (US technician training) [P] — [TechForce Ford Fund](https://techforce.org/fordfund/); "Ford Blue Oval" scholarships in the UK/Australia are administered by individual universities (e.g., [Swinburne](https://swinburne.edu.au/study/options/scholarships/374/ford-blue-oval-scholarship)). None are for Mexico-based students.

### Inferences
- Treat the US, German and Turkish Ford programs as non-options for summer 2027 unless Esteban obtains independent work authorization; Ford Mexico (GTBC) is the only Ford route realistically open to him.
- Sponsored global exposure at Ford is more likely after hire (employees at GTBC collaborate with US/German/UK teams; see [Ford "Mexican engineering around the world"](https://www.at.ford.com/us/en/home/news-media/news/2024/9/mexican-engineering-around-the-world-part-1.html), which describes collaboration but names no formal rotation).

### Gaps
- No Ford India / China intern program data gathered; likely irrelevant for a Mexican citizen but not verified.
- Whether Ford's US Summer Internship ever sponsors visas for Mexican citizens: not found.
- Ford Otosan and Ford-Werke eligibility for non-EU/non-Turkish applicants: not found.

## Q6. Mexican or international scholarships/programs that specifically fund internships at companies

### Takeaway
No Mexican public/private scholarship that funds internships "at big companies for final-year students" was found. The nearest options are IAESTE México (paid technical internships abroad, run from the Instituto Tecnológico de Durango) and DAAD RISE Professional (eligibility for Mexicans unclear, likely excluded). Both need verification.

### Cited Findings
- IAESTE México: national office at Instituto Tecnológico de Durango (Coordinación de Lenguas Extranjeras), open to students from anywhere in Mexico; present in 80+ countries; contact WhatsApp 618 100 8756. [N] — [Posta Durango](https://www.posta.com.mx/durango/asi-puedes-estudiar-por-intercambio-en-el-extranjero-si-eres-de-durango/vl2210064). A search summary (UNVERIFIED) said placements last 2-3 months (up to 6), with medical insurance and living support, and an annual call. EU youth portal confirms IAESTE internships are paid in general. [P] — [Youth Portal IAESTE](https://youth.europa.eu/go-abroad/traineeships/iaeste-exchange-programme_es)
- DAAD RISE Professional: places students/graduates in German companies and research institutions; reported stipend EUR 861/month for 1.5-6 months in one guide; older/official descriptions limit it to North American, British and Irish students and mainly master's/PhD; conflicting sources on Mexico. [P/A] — [DAAD RISE Professional](https://www.daad.de/rise-pro); [DAAD "what applicants need to know"](https://www.daad.de/rise/en/rise-professional/find-an-internship/what-applicants-need-to-know/)
- DAAD KOSPIE (study+internship for Latin American engineering students): mentioned in a search summary only; Mexico eligibility unconfirmed.
- Company scholarships tied to specific universities: FEMSA-UNAM, Telmex-Telcel (IPN) — university-restricted, not internship funding. [N] — [UNAM FEMSA convocatoria](https://www.derecho.unam.mx/becas/images/conv/Conv-FEMSA2026-1.pdf)
- Company-funded Ford/Nissan/BMW scholarships found are for school-level or technical (TSU/dual) programs, not for final-year university internships (see Q1, Q3).

### Inferences
- In practice, in Mexico the paid internship itself (Ford Champs, GM becario, BMW, VW Talento Joven) is the "beca"; no separate scholarship funds it.
- IAESTE is the most practical funded-abroad route for engineering undergrads, but it matches students by company request and field, so it should be checked with the Durango office.

### Gaps
- No official IAESTE México site was reached; term lengths, deadlines and stipend unconfirmed.
