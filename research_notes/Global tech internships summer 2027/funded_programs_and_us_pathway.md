# Funded / sponsored international internship, exchange and summer-research programs for a Mexican undergraduate (summer 2027) and the realistic US J-1 intern pathway

Research date: 30 Oct 2026 (target: summer 2027). Candidate: UTM Computer Engineering, 3rd year in summer 2027, GPA 9.0/10, ~4 semesters left after summer 2027. Method note: most facts below were read from fetched primary pages; items marked "(search summary only)" come from a web-search snippet and were not opened in full. Items marked "(third-party)" come from aggregator or news sites and should be re-checked on the official page. Many programs have not yet published 2027 dates; where I extrapolate from the 2026 cycle I say so.

## 1. IAESTE: does Mexico have an active committee, and how could a UTM student join?

### Takeaway
Mexico is listed on IAESTE's official country list as "Mexico, ITD", and a May 2026 news article places the national IAESTE Mexico office at the Instituto Tecnológico de Durango (ITD) with the call open to students from any Mexican university, so a UTM student can apply through ITD rather than through a UTM local committee. No Mexican fee, deadline or selection process is published anywhere I could reach, so the first step is a direct message to ITD. IAESTE internships are paid by the host and are the best-documented route to Switzerland, Austria, China and others.

### Cited Findings
- IAESTE's official country list shows "Mexico, ITD" (the page does not define "ITD" and gives no Mexico contact) — [IAESTE member countries](https://iaeste.org/countries)
- On IAESTE's home page, Mexico appears only in the internship filter as "Mexico, ITD (0)", i.e. no internships hosted in Mexico were listed at fetch time — [IAESTE home](https://iaeste.org)
- National headquarters of IAESTE Mexico is in the Instituto Tecnológico de Durango, Coordinación de Lenguas Extranjeras; the call is open to any professional-level (licenciatura/engineering) student in Mexico, not just Durango; contact by WhatsApp 618 100 8756; the article (30 May 2026) says the call "remains open" but gives no requirements, dates or costs — [Posta Durango, 30 May 2026](https://www.posta.com.mx/durango/asi-puedes-estudiar-por-intercambio-en-el-extranjero-si-eres-de-durango/vl2210064)
- ITD's accountability report lists IAESTE in its glossary and a third-party listing shows MX-2026 offers tagged to ITD with June-October 2026 windows (third-party; not an official IAESTE Mexico page) — [ITD informe 2019-2024](https://itdurango.edu.mx/descargas/sexenal.pdf); [third-party IAESTE listing mirror](https://merchopstraining.inspireli.com/en/education/iaeste-internships?vp-page=3)
- Eligibility: currently enrolled (or graduated up to one year) in a Bachelor's, Master's or PhD; mostly STEM; listed durations range from about 12 to 105 weeks; on-site internships are paid, with examples of 2,500 CHF/month (Switzerland), 2,350 EUR (Austria), 1,200 EUR (France), 3,000 CNY (China); remote internships are also offered; each offer shows its own application deadline and date window (e.g. 01.09.2026-01.07.2027 for many Austrian offers) — [IAESTE for students](https://iaeste.org/students)
- Browsable offers with deadlines: [IAESTE internships](https://iaeste.org/internships); remote only: [IAESTE remote internships](https://iaeste.org/internships?utf8=✓&ref_no=&internship_type=remote&sort=deadline_at)
- Route for students from non-member countries (use only if Mexico's committee cannot place you): register on the Exchange Platform choosing "Non-Member Country", complete profile, **pay a registration fee (amount not stated; FEE FLAG)**, then apply; decision expected within 8 weeks of nomination; confirm acceptance within 2 weeks; start visa/work-permit process early; buy health insurance; feedback form within 4 months — [IAESTE non-member students](https://iaeste.org/student-nonmembers). Mexico also appears in the "Jump To Country" list on that page, so Mexico's exact status (member vs. cooperating institution vs. non-member route) is not clear from IAESTE's own pages.
- How a national committee works, as documented by the Peruvian committee (not Mexico): IAESTE is reciprocity-based (the country must secure a placement for an incoming foreign student per outgoing student); selection is done by the national committee (top third of class, intermediate English, volunteering with IAESTE); typical internships 2-3 months, up to 6-12 months; foreign trainees arrive June-September; the trainee pays airfare; the incoming trainee's practitioner visa is coordinated through IAESTE; "reserved offers" (employer already has a candidate) may carry an admin fee depending on the country; collaborating employers listed include ABB Schweiz, ETH Zurich, Oracle Labs, Apple, Siemens, Sony CSL, Toyota Motor Europe — [UDEP IAESTE FAQ (Peru), 2026](https://www.udep.edu.pe/internacional/wp-content/uploads/sites/27/2026/08/IAESTE-Preguntas-frecuentes-Word.pdf)
- Latin American committees run national selection in the autumn before the summer (e.g. Argentine universities announce calls closing in late Nov/early Dec) (search-result snippet only, year not confirmed) — [UNL call thread](https://listas.unl.edu.ar/mailman3/hyperkitty/list/alumnos-fich@listas.unl.edu.ar/thread/QP5WX6WWK6BRQVLN7GOXPDMPZXW7PUQJ/)
- IAESTE also lists "Computer Engineering (Ing. en Computación)" and telecommunications among eligible fields — [UDEP IAESTE FAQ (Peru)](https://www.udep.edu.pe/internacional/wp-content/uploads/sites/27/2026/08/IAESTE-Preguntas-frecuentes-Word.pdf)

### Inferences
- A UTM student probably cannot rely on a UTM local committee (no evidence UTM is an IAESTE institution); applying directly through ITD's national office is the likely route, and the Peruvian-style rules (English level, top-third GPA, reciprocity, volunteer work) probably apply in some form. His 9.0 GPA and advanced English fit.
- If Mexico's committee has few outgoing slots or no reciprocal incoming offers, IAESTE's own internship search (and the non-member route, with a fee) are the fallback; the fee amount must be confirmed.
- Because national selection usually precedes the platform's offer rounds in the autumn/winter, contacting ITD in November 2026 is time-critical for summer 2027.

### Gaps
- No official IAESTE Mexico website, application timeline, fee, or list of Mexican partner universities was found; whether UTM has any IAESTE ties is unknown.
- Whether Mexico is a full member, cooperating institution or non-member in IAESTE's structure is unverified ("ITD" is undefined on iaeste.org).
- Deadlines for 2027 exchanges: not published/found. How many Swiss/Swedish/Irish/Chinese computing offers exist for summer 2027 could not be counted (the offer list is behind the Exchange Platform and was not browsed).
- Whether IAESTE handles work permits for Mexican outgoing students in each destination: the IAESTE pages say only to start the process early; the Peruvian FAQ says incoming trainees' visas are coordinated through IAESTE.

## 2. AIESEC Global Talent / Global Teacher from Mexico: costs, legitimacy, relevance to tech

### Takeaway
I could not verify any current AIESEC México Global Talent fee, placement list or student cost from reliable sources; only fragments exist. AIESEC does list tech internships (including software engineering) through its Mexico entity, but there is no evidence of a scholarship, and at least one AIESEC entity charges an administrative fee. Treat as a possible paid-placement fallback, not a funded program.

### Cited Findings
- AIESEC México has a listed "Global Software Engineer Internship" under Global Talent that mentions accommodation, transport support and global exposure, but no cost (third-party job aggregator) — [Vaia listing](https://talents.vaia.com/companies/aiesec-mexico/global-software-engineer-internship-94773396/)
- An AIESEC info-session deck from another country describes a Global Talent administrative fee that covers company search, CV advice and interview preparation (this is not the Mexican fee; search summary only) — [FIB-UPC AIESEC info session](https://www.fib.upc.edu/sites/fib/files/documents/mobilitat/aiesec_info_session_gt_it-1.pdf)
- Older (2014) sources say Global Talent internships last 3-12 months, are paid according to the host-country cost of living, and that IT roles were among the most demanded; another source says AIESEC itself gives no scholarships (search summary only; old) — [Trabajar por el Mundo](https://trabajarporelmundo.org/?p=15937); [UDEP 2014](https://www.udep.edu.pe/hoy/2014/05/objetivo-practicas-en-el-extranjero/)
- A user-review page for AIESEC en México exists (not read) — [Trustburn](https://trustburn.com/reviews/aiesec-en-m-xico)

### Inferences
- Because AIESEC Global Talent is a placement service that often charges the student an admin fee and does not itself fund, it is lower priority than IAESTE, EPFL, CERN, or open-source paid programs. Quality of tech placements varies by host and country; verify the host, pay, and visa responsibility in writing before paying anything.

### Gaps
- Official AIESEC México pricing, Global Talent catalogue and legitimacy/complaint history were not retrieved (aiesec.org.mx was not fetched). Global Teacher not researched; it is not relevant to a software internship.

## 3. Mexican government funding (SECIHTI, SEP, Oaxaca, Programa Delfín, AMEXCID, COMEXUS/Fulbright)

### Takeaway
I found no federal or Oaxaca program that funds an undergraduate's international summer internship in Europe or Asia. SECIHTI mobility support targets national postgraduate scholarship holders; COMEXUS Fulbright-García Robles calls found are graduate only; Programa Delfín does include some international stays but mostly in Latin America and only for students of affiliated institutions (UTM's membership is unconfirmed). The foreign-government degree scholarships channelled through AMEXCID (e.g. China) are degree programs, not internships.

### Cited Findings
- **Programa Delfín**: created 1995, 346 institutions, with a "Convocatoria 2026" for the Verano de la Investigación; the homepage does not mention international stays, does not list member institutions, and gives no 2027 dates — [Programa Delfín](https://programadelfin.org.mx/)
- Delfín 2026 call (as relayed by a Colombian university): 7-week stays at partner universities nationally and internationally; destinations listed: Brazil, Colombia, Costa Rica, Ecuador, Mexico, Nicaragua, Panama, Peru, United States; in-person and virtual modalities; usual limits: no more than two prior Delfín summers, and often at least 50% of credits — [UR Global Delfín call](https://urosario.edu.co/sites/default/files/2026-02/convocatoria-delfin-ur-global.pdf)
- Delfín 2026 timeline at IPN: applications 10 Feb-13 Mar 2026, stay 8 Jun-24 Jul 2026, results 6 May 2026; each institution sets its own internal deadline — [UPIITA/IPN Delfín 2026](https://www.upiita.ipn.mx/adjuntos/apoyos%20educativos/2026/delfin2026.pdf); [UNISON Delfín 2026](https://movilidad.unison.mx/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Convocatoria_Programa_Delfin_2026.pdf)
- Only students of Delfín-affiliated institutions can apply; no source I found lists UTM as a member (search summary; membership roster not retrieved) — [Delfín statutes 2022-2025](https://www.programadelfin.org.mx/recursos/documentos/delfin23-estatutos2022-2025.pdf)
- **SECIHTI**: the 2026 "Apoyos Complementarios de Movilidad" call (abroad, national, sectors of interest, double degree) was published around 23 Feb 2026; it supplements existing SECIHTI national scholarship holders (postgraduate) with stipends (search summary; third-party amounts conflict, e.g. 8,000 MXN/month for double degree vs 22,660 MXN for abroad mobility) — [SECIHTI Convocatoria AC Movilidad 2026](https://secihti.mx/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Convocatoria_AC_Movilidad_2026-20260223T173424.pdf); [SECIHTI TdR Movilidad 2026](https://secihti.mx/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/TdR_Movilidad_2026-20260223T173624.pdf); [Tesify summary (third-party)](https://tesify.es/becas-secihti-movilidad-espana-2026). UTM reposted the 2025 edition in its bulletin — [UTM Boletín Universitario](https://www.utm.mx/web/cultura/publicaciones/boletin_universitario)
- **SEP**: a municipal call (Celaya) refers to SEP's "Movilidad Estudiantil Nacional e Internacional, de Concursos y Viajes Académicos 2026" programme guidelines; I could not open the SEP guidelines (search summary only) — [Celaya call, Feb 2026](https://www.celaya.gob.mx/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Convocatoria-Becas-Movilidad-estudiantil-Feb-2026.pdf)
- **COMEXUS / Fulbright-García Robles**: the call found is graduate (posgrado) for studies starting August 2027, with an application window reported as 1 Sep 2025-31 Jan 2026 (closed); no undergraduate option found — [COMEXUS Posgrado Regular call](https://comexus.org.mx/documentos/becas/ConvocatoriaPosgradoRegular_sitioweb_2026-08-06_H_22-32-12.pdf)
- **AMEXCID / foreign governments**: AMEXCID relays foreign-government scholarship calls to Mexicans; the Chinese Government Scholarship 2026-27 opened in Nov 2025 with an online deadline of 16 Jan (bachelor's included; pre-admission letter from a Chinese university required; full tuition, housing, monthly stipend) (news/third-party) — [Mexico Solidarity summary](https://mexicosolidarity.com/peoples-republic-of-china-announces-2026-full-scholarship-program-for-mexican-citizens); [Viveusa](https://www.viveusa.mx/educacion/la-beca-todo-pagado-para-mexicanos-que-quieren-estudiar-y-vivir-en-china-requisitos/); [SRE press note](https://www.gob.mx/sre/prensa/166624)
- UTM is a state public university in the SUNEO Oaxaca system — [Wikipedia: UTM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technological_University_of_the_Mixteca); UTM's own site returned no mobility page to my fetches ([utm.mx](https://www.utm.mx/nuestrauniversidad_eng.html))

### Inferences
- Delfín would only help if UTM is an affiliated institution and the candidate would accept Latin American or US destinations (the cited destination list contains no European or Asian country); ask UTM's research/vinculación office. A 2027 call would probably follow the 2026 rhythm (applications Feb-Mar, stay June-July), but this is an extrapolation.
- SECIHTI mobility money is not a route for an undergraduate; it becomes relevant only if he later holds a SECIHTI postgraduate scholarship.
- For a 2027 Chinese-government route, the next call would likely appear Nov 2026-Jan 2027 (extrapolation from the 2025-26 timing), but that funds degree study (long programs), not a summer internship.

### Gaps
- Not found: any Oaxaca state-government international-stay scholarship for undergraduates; any SEP federal international-mobility call for 2027; UTM's own international-mobility office/page and its agreements; whether the old Proyecta 100,000/Jóvenes en Acción programs still run (not researched/verified); Delfín 2027 dates and the official affiliated-institution list.
- No undergraduate-level summer research program through COMEXUS was found; a separate undergraduate State Department program (e.g. SUSI) was not researched.

## 4. Private/corporate scholarships and remote programs (Santander, Telmex-Telcel, BBVA, Google, Microsoft, Meta, Amazon, Intel, MLH, LFX, GSoC, Outreachy, RISC-V)

### Takeaway
The only corporate programs I could confirm that reach a Mexican undergraduate are the remote, paid open-source programs: Google Summer of Code (Mexico stipend 750/1,500/3,000 USD by project size), LFX Mentorship (3,500 USD for Mexico under the post-July-2026 model), Outreachy (7,000 USD) and the RISC-V mentorship (via LFX), plus the MLH Fellowship (stipend amount unpublished). The US-centred internship pipelines (Google STEP, Microsoft Explore, Meta University) and Santander mobility awards either target US/Canada students or are tied to specific universities, and I found no confirmed access for a UTM student.

### Cited Findings
#### Google Summer of Code (GSoC)
- 2026 schedule: contributor applications 16-31 Mar 2026; accepted projects announced 30 Apr; community bonding 1-24 May; coding from 25 May; midterm 6-10 Jul; standard final submissions 17-24 Aug; extended projects to 2 Nov; page covers 2026 only, 2027 dates not yet published and "can shift a bit each year" — [GSoC timeline](https://developers.google.com/open-source/gsoc/timeline)
- Mexico stipends: 750 USD (small), 1,500 USD (medium), 3,000 USD (large); paid 45% at first evaluation and 55% at final (2026 dates 11 Jul / 1 Sep) — [GSoC stipend table](https://developers.google.com/open-source/gsoc/help/student-stipends)
- Eligibility: 18+, a student or open-source beginner, not resident in a US-embargoed country (Russia, Belarus, DNR/LNR excluded), eligible to work in country of residence; up to three proposals, one accepted; a person accepted more than once before is ineligible — [GSoC FAQ](https://developers.google.com/open-source/gsoc/faq)
- Organization list for 2026 and program site (registration closed): [GSoC home](https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com/)

#### Linux Foundation LFX Mentorship (includes RISC-V International)
- For programs starting on or after 1 Jul 2026, stipends range from 1,000 to 6,600 USD using a PPP formula from a 6,000 USD baseline; many projects are unpaid — [Linux Foundation blog](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/blog/strengthening-lfx-mentoring-a-fairer-stipend-model-and-a-shared-standard-of-excellence)
- Mexico: 3,500 USD — [LFX stipend table](https://docs.linuxfoundation.org/lfx/mentorship/mentee-stipends/total-stipend-amount)
- RISC-V mentorship (3 months, ~30 h/week, funded by RISC-V International members): Spring 2027 term 1 Mar-31 May 2027, mentee applications 8 Jan-7 Feb 2027, mentor proposals through 20 Nov 2026; Summer 2026 ran 1 Jun-31 Aug (mentee window 14 Apr-17 May 2026); Fall 2026 window closed 5 Aug; only the first selected mentee on a project gets the stipend, others may join unpaid; no Summer 2027 dates yet; apply through the RISC-V job board and LFX — [RISC-V mentorship](https://riscv.org/community/mentorship/); [LFX mentorship docs](https://docs.linuxfoundation.org/lfx/mentorship)

#### Outreachy
- Stipend 7,000 USD for a 3-month internship, 30 h/week; the December 2026 round (7 Dec 2026-8 Mar 2027) is already past its initial-application deadline (31 Aug 2026) and is in its contribution period (12 Oct-9 Nov 2026), so it is not a summer option; open to people facing underrepresentation or systemic bias in their country's tech industry; students must have 42 consecutive days free of classes and exams; Northern-Hemisphere students apply to the May-August cohort; past Outreachy, OPW and **GSoC interns are ineligible**; no concurrent internships or full-time job; 2027 dates not yet published; Mexican residents not excluded by the text but confirm with organizers — [Outreachy applicant docs](https://www.outreachy.org/docs/applicant/)

#### MLH Fellowship
- Fully remote 12-week fellowship, tracks: Software Engineering, Production Engineering (SRE), Web3 Engineering; educational stipend (amount not stated on the page); no 2026-27 cohort dates or country eligibility listed — [MLH Fellowship](https://fellowship.mlh.com/)

#### Corporate scholarships and pipelines
- Fundación Telmex-Telcel: 2025 call (reposted by UTM) for licenciatura/maestría/doctorado students with minimum average 8.5, up to 5,000 scholarships, registration 25 Aug-30 Sep 2025, final deadline 3 Oct 2025; no international mobility mentioned; a 2026 edition was not found — [UTM bulletin: Beca Telmex 2025](https://www.utm.mx/web/cultura/publicaciones/boletin_universitario/2025-08-27-beca-telmex)
- Santander mobility: the Santander-UNAM semester mobility call (UNAM students only, about 38 places, destinations including US, UK, Spain, Colombia, Peru, Argentina, registration on Santander Open Academy; Aug-Sep 2025 window) — [Expansión, 25 Aug 2025](https://expansion.mx/carrera/2025/08/25/unam-y-santander-becas-de-movilidad-solo-hay-38-lugares); [UNAM call PDF](https://www.becas.unam.mx/bodega/2025/08/ConvMovIntSem2026-2.pdf). Semester exchange, not an internship; whether UTM participates in Santander mobility awards is unconfirmed.
- Google Generation Scholarship: North America round closed (IIE), APAC round deadline was 14 Aug 2025; no Latin America edition was found (a claim that Latin American students are eligible appears on one aggregator and is unverified) — [IIE Generation Google (North America)](https://www.iie.org/programs/generationgoogle/generation-google-scholarship-canada-united-states-north-america); [Buddy4Study APAC](https://www.buddy4study.com/article/generation-google-scholarship)
- Google in Mexico: 30,000 Google Career Certificate scholarships through SEP (AI and cybersecurity) are training, not internships — [Mexico News Daily](https://mexiconewsdaily.com/news/google-digital-skills-training-women)
- Google STEP (first/second-year undergraduates), Microsoft Explore and Meta University: only third-party descriptions found (STEP aimed at first/second-years returning to a Bachelor's for at least two more years; Meta University for sophomores from underrepresented groups and reportedly requires US work authorization) — [Simplify list of underclassmen programs](https://simplify.jobs/blog/25-software-engineering-programs-for-underclassmen); [CodePath early internship programs](https://books.codepath.org/student-handbook/internship-and-job-search-strategy/early-internship-programs)
- Ford: no Ford program giving Mexican students a US internship was found. Ford programs in Mexico include Ford Driving Dreams (scholarships), "Educación en Movimiento" (an extra 2 million USD for scholarships) and "Innovation Partners" (six-month projects at the Cuautitlán plant with Universidad Anáhuac students) — [Cluster Industrial: Innovation Partners](https://clusterindustrial.com.mx/ford-reta-al-talento-universitario-a-transformar-la-industria-automotriz-mediante-el-programa-innovation-partners/); [El Financiero, 9 Jul 2025](https://www.elfinanciero.com.mx/transporte-y-movilidad/2025/07/09/ford-celebra-100-anos-en-mexico-con-inversion-educativa-y-expansion-tecnologica/); [CarNews: Ford Driving Dreams](https://www.carnews.com.mx/?p=52656)

### Inferences
- Only one of GSoC / LFX / Outreachy / MLH can realistically fill the summer because Outreachy bars concurrent internships and requires 30 h/week; since Outreachy excludes past GSoC interns while GSoC's FAQ only bars people accepted more than once, the order Outreachy then GSoC is allowed and GSoC then Outreachy is not.
- Mexico's GSoC stipend (as low as 750 USD) is well below LFX (3,500 USD) and Outreachy (7,000 USD); the LFX and RISC-V programs fit his RISC-V CPU project best. If dates repeat the 2026 pattern, the GSoC 2027 contributor window would be roughly mid-to-late March 2027 and the RISC-V summer mentee window roughly mid-April to mid-May 2027 (extrapolation; not published).
- The "Ford scholarship-for-internship" the user heard of could not be matched to a documented program; the closest verifiable items are Ford Driving Dreams scholarships and the Cuautitlán internship program (neither involves a US stay).

### Gaps
- Not verified from official pages: Santander Becas Tecnología/other Santander schemes open to UTM, Fundación BBVA programs, Amazon Future Engineer outside the US, Intel, NVIDIA and Microsoft Leap/Explore eligibility for Mexico-based students; Telmex-Telcel 2026 call; MLH stipend amount and eligibility FAQ; Outreachy 2027 dates and whether a Oaxaca-based Mexican applicant satisfies the underrepresentation criterion; GSoC 2027 dates.

## 5. Foreign-government and university programs open to Mexicans (Switzerland, Sweden, Ireland, China, DAAD RISE, Mitacs Globalink, CERN)

### Takeaway
For a third-year undergraduate the best verified, funded options with open or imminent deadlines are Summer@EPFL (Switzerland, deadline 29 Nov 2026, CHF 1,800/month + travel refund) and CERN's summer programs (applications open Nov 2026). DAAD RISE Germany is open now (deadline 30 Nov 2026) but its country eligibility for Mexico is unclear. The Swiss Government Excellence research scholarships require a Master's; Mitacs Globalink for summer 2027 is closed and he would probably not meet its "1-3 semesters remaining" rule; I found no Swedish, Irish or Chinese undergraduate internship scholarship for Mexicans.

### Cited Findings
#### Switzerland
- **Summer@EPFL (School of Computer and Communication Sciences)**: Bachelor/Master students in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Telecommunications or Electrical Engineering; Bachelor's applicants must have completed year 1 (in at least 2nd year); living allowance CHF 1,800/month pro rata, refund of 2nd-class train or economy airfare; 2-3 months, usually May-September, flexible; application deadline Sunday closest to 1 Dec = **29 Nov 2026**; decisions before 25 Jan; offered to international students, no nationality restriction listed; no application fee stated — [Summer@EPFL](https://summer.epfl.ch/)
- **ETH Zurich Student Summer Research Fellowship (D-INFK)**: open to all students worldwide except those enrolled at ETH; the official page gives no stipend, dates or deadline — [ETH D-INFK SSRF](https://inf.ethz.ch/studies/summer-research-fellowship.html). Third-party sources report a CHF 4,000 stipend and 30 Jun-29 Aug dates for 2025 and eligibility variants (unverified) — [WUR notice](https://wur.nl/en/show/eth-student-sumer-research-fellowship-2025-eth-ssrf.htm)
- **CERN Summer Student Programme**: 2026 cohort of 8-13 weeks chosen from more than 21,000 applicants; Member/Associate Member State students use standard routes while non-Member State students apply through a dedicated route; applications for 2027 open in November 2026 — [CERN EP news](https://ep-news.web.cern.ch/one-summer-a-world-of-possibilities-cerns-summer-student-programme-2026/); the non-Member State programme is aimed at advanced undergraduates/early graduates in physics, computing and engineering and is partly funded by home governments and universities (search summary only) — [NMS summer student page](http://nms.web.cern.ch/)
- **CERN openlab summer student programme (computing; 2026 rules)**: Bachelor or Master students, at least 6 semesters of full-time study completed by summer 2026, must stay enrolled, computer science/maths/engineering/physics, all nationalities welcome including Non-Member States, 9 weeks, 93 CHF/day net, lump-sum travel allowance, no prior CERN summer student status, decisions late March/early April, start 22 or 29 June — [openlab FAQ](https://openlab.cern/faqs-summer-student-programme/)
- **Swiss Government Excellence Scholarships 2027-28**: research scholarships target early-career researchers who already hold a Master's; maximum age 35; CHF 2,450/month; country list published August 2026 (183 countries, Mexico not confirmed); applying is free — [SBFI scholarships](https://www.sbfi.admin.ch/scholarships_en)
- IAESTE Switzerland example pay: 2,500 CHF/month — [IAESTE for students](https://iaeste.org/students)

#### Germany / Canada (outside the preferred list)
- **DAAD RISE Germany 2027**: undergraduates from "North American, British and Irish universities" in biology, chemistry, physics, earth sciences, engineering or closely related fields; 3-month summer research internship with a monthly stipend; applications 15 Oct-30 Nov 2026 (23:59 CET), reference letters due 15 Dec 2026, placements announced Feb/Mar 2027; the page lists no individual countries and does not confirm Mexico — [DAAD RISE Germany](https://www.daad.de/rise/en/rise-germany/). A secondary guide describes it as for North America, UK and Ireland only — [Opportunity Inside](https://opportunityinside.com/scholarships/daad-rise-germany-2027.html)
- **DAAD RISE Worldwide** sends students enrolled at German universities abroad, so it does not apply to a UTM student — [TU Darmstadt notice](https://www.tu-darmstadt.de/universitaet/international/international_news_detail_575488.en.jsp)
- **Mitacs Globalink Research Internship (Canada), summer 2027**: Mexico is an eligible country (Mexican citizenship; minimum grade 80% or equivalent; all Mexican universities eligible); requires at least two years of undergraduate study done with one to three semesters remaining as of fall 2027; 12 weeks, May-Oct 2027; funding covers airfare contribution, housing, food/incidentals, enrollment fee, health insurance; **the summer 2027 student call has concluded**; Tec de Monterrey students pay a 2,500 USD contribution (FEE FLAG, only ITESM) — [Mitacs GRI students](https://www.mitacs.ca/our-programs/globalink-research-internship-students/). The student window ran roughly Aug 5-Sep 16, 2026 for Canada-bound applicants — [TU Darmstadt notice](https://www.tu-darmstadt.de/universitaet/international/international_news_detail_575488.en.jsp). ## 6. US pathway: the J-1 Intern visa (requirements, sponsors, DS-7002, costs, timeline)

### Takeaway
A Mexican undergraduate can legally intern in the US on a J-1 Intern visa if he is currently enrolled at a foreign post-secondary school (or graduated within 12 months), has a US host company that signs a DS-7002 training plan, and is accepted by a State-Department-designated sponsor that issues the DS-2019. The student pays sponsor fees plus government fees (roughly 1,300-1,900 USD before insurance for a short program with Cultural Vistas), the host has to find or accept him first, and a visa interview in person is now required.

### Cited Findings
- Definition and eligibility: foreign students currently enrolled in and pursuing studies at a degree- or certificate-granting post-secondary institution outside the US, or graduates within 12 months before the program start; interns cannot take unskilled or casual labor, childcare/elder care, patient-care roles, or positions with more than 20% clerical work — [J-1 Intern category (State Dept.)](https://j1visa.state.gov/programs/intern)
- Host/DS-7002: hosts sign the Training/Internship Placement Plan (DS-7002), must provide continuous on-site supervision and structured learning; sponsors must do site visits for new hosts with fewer than 25 employees and less than 3 million USD revenue, collect EIN and proof of workers' compensation insurance, and obtain evaluations (midpoint and final if over six months; final only if shorter) — [J-1 Intern category (State Dept.)](https://j1visa.state.gov/programs/intern)
- The student must present a completed DS-7002 at the visa interview; the sponsor issues DS-2019 only after DS-7002 is complete; the host department usually fills in the DS-7002 — [Wichita State DS-7002 guide](https://www.wichita.edu/admissions/international/documents/Guide_to_Form_DS-7002.docx); [Central Washington University](https://www.cwu.edu/academics/international-programs/isss/visa-information/j-1-student-interns)
- Duration: up to 12 months per foreign degree level for interns (18 months for trainees); programs typically run 3 weeks to 12 months — [UConn J-1 student intern program](https://international.global.uconn.edu/department-information/department-information-hosting-an-exchange-visitor/student-intern-program-j-1-exchange-visitor/); [Cultural Vistas fee page](https://culturalvistas.org/j-1-fees-intern-trainee-research-scholar-specialist)
- University guidance adds: good academic standing at the home school, internship must relate to the current degree, return to the home program afterward, proof of funds, English proficiency, health insurance meeting State Dept. minimums (university J-1 pages; search summary) — [Brandeis](https://www.brandeis.edu/isso/scholars-staff/j-1-intern.html); [GWU DS-2019 request](https://internationalservices.gwu.edu/sites/g/files/zaxdzs2486/f/J-1%20Intern%20DS-2019%20Request_DS-7002_Intl%20School%20Cert..pdf)
- **Costs (Cultural Vistas, a designated sponsor; FEE FLAG)**: student program fee 900 USD (up to 3 months), 1,125 USD (up to 6), 1,375 USD (up to 12); SEVIS I-901 220 USD (paid by the sponsor on the participant's behalf); health insurance 60 USD/month (Standard Lite) or 100 USD/month (Enhanced); visa interview fee 185 USD; processing 4-6 weeks (expedited 5 or 10 business days for an extra 1,500 or 1,350 USD); new host orientation 300 USD (cannot be paid by the J-1 candidate); site visit 300 USD (expedited 1,500 USD) — [Cultural Vistas J-1 fees](https://culturalvistas.org/j-1-fees-intern-trainee-research-scholar-specialist)
- Other sponsors charge differently: FACC-NY lists 1,500 USD for a non-VIE intern, and some sponsors charge a 300-500 USD site visit fee (search summary); the visa fee is quoted as 185 USD or 160 USD by different sites (conflict) — [FACC-NY visa fees](https://faccnyc.org/about-visa/visa-fees-candidates/); [InterExchange](https://www.interexchange.org/?p=5197)
- Interview rules: since 2 Sep 2025 the interview waiver is no longer available for most nonimmigrant applicants; J-1 is not among the remaining waiver categories (A, G, NATO and a few others), and applicants should normally apply in their country of nationality or residence — [State Dept. interview waiver update, 18 Sep 2025](https://travel.state.gov/en/newsroom/visas/2025/interview-waiver-update-sept-18-2025.html); [CU Boulder ISSS notice](https://www.colorado.edu/isss/node/3461)
- Regulatory risk: a proposed State Department rule (published 30 Jul 2026, 91 FR 48021) would tighten J-1 termination, extension and reinstatement rules; the search found no 2026 rule aimed specifically at intern/trainee categories (summary from law-firm and university notices; the Federal Register text was not read) — [Yale OISS summary](https://oiss.yale.edu/news/state-department-proposes-changes-to-j-1-exchange-visitor-program-regulations)
- Designated sponsors that handle J-1 interns and appear in search results: Cultural Vistas, InterExchange (Career Training USA), FACC and others; sponsor lists are on [j1visa.state.gov](https://j1visa.state.gov/programs/intern) and sponsor sites — [InterExchange career training](https://www.interexchange.org/programs/career-training/university-advisor-resources/)

### Inferences
- The J-1 route is an "employer-first" route: it does not provide a scholarship or placement by itself. The student normally needs a US company willing to host (and complete the DS-7002), then a sponsor; total out-of-pocket for a 3-month stay is roughly 900-1,000 USD sponsor fee + 185 USD visa + insurance (about 180-300 USD) + airfare and living costs unless the host pays a stipend.
- Timeline backwards from a June 2027 start: host offer by about Feb-Mar 2027; sponsor application at least 4-6 weeks before the DS-2019 is needed; then visa appointment (in-person, in Mexico) plus a buffer; so sponsor paperwork should start by about March-April 2027.
- Because J-1 interns must be currently enrolled abroad, the candidate stays eligible through summer 2027 and the following years of his degree; the 12-month cap per degree level means a summer 2027 internship does not exhaust a later one.

### Gaps
- No official State Dept. page on the maximum duration; the 12-month figure comes from sponsor/university pages.
- Visa appointment wait times at the US Embassy/consulates in Mexico, and the Oaxaca-relevant consulate, were not researched.
- The proposed 30 Jul 2026 rule text was not read; its effect on interns is unverified.
