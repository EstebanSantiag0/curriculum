## 1. Oracle Labs internships (Zurich and other hubs): are bachelor-level interns from foreign universities accepted? Areas, duration, stipend, process, any 2027 posting?

### Takeaway
Oracle Labs (research arm) runs rolling, project-based internships for "students and recent graduates" in hubs in Austria, Czech Republic, Switzerland (Zurich, Prime Tower) and Morocco. The Zurich/Database AI Research postings (GraalVM, Graal Cloud Service, Micronaut) are the best fit for Esteban's profile (Java, C/C++, Compilers, OS, DBs): they typically last 6 months, are flexible on duration and are applied for by emailing a CV, not through the careers site. No 2027-dated Oracle Labs posting exists yet, and none of the sources state a degree level or a work-permit policy for non-EU/EFTA interns, which is the main open risk for Switzerland.
