## 1. Switzerland: funded summer programmes and corporate/lab options open to him

### Takeaway
Switzerland's realistic doors are the funded research fellowships/studentships (ETH SSRF, EPFL E3, Summer@EPFL, CERN Summer Student and openlab), which are open to non-EU students at any university and solve the permit problem internally. Corporate internships (Google, IBM, etc.) are essentially closed to him as an enrolled-in-Mexico, voluntary-internship candidate. Two EPFL deadlines (E3 on 1 Nov 2026, Summer@EPFL on 29 Nov 2026) are immediate; the ETH SSRF window is open now (1 Nov to 16 Dec 2026).

### Cited Findings

## 2. Switzerland: work-permit and immigration rules for non-EU interns enrolled abroad

### Takeaway
There is no general permit for a voluntary summer internship for a Mexican student. Switzerland's trainee ("stagiaire") agreements do not include Mexico and also require a completed degree. The only internship route for a student enrolled abroad is a *mandatory* internship (confirmed in writing by the home university), typically via IAESTE/AIESEC, quota-limited and capped at 12 months. Fellowships/studentships (ETH, EPFL, CERN) avoid this problem.

### Cited Findings
- Canton of Zurich (Amt fuer Wirtschaft): foreign students enrolled at a foreign university or university of applied sciences may do a Praktikum in Switzerland **only if it is mandatory** for their degree; stay limited to 12 months in total and only once; permits issued only to firms where short-term foreign employees do not exceed 25% of the workforce; applications generally via placement organisations such as IAESTE or AIESEC; a letter from the foreign university confirming enrolment and that the internship is mandatory is required; local/industry pay and working conditions must be observed — [Kanton Zurich: Studierende und Praktikanten](https://www.zh.ch/de/wirtschaft-arbeit/erwerbstaetigkeit-auslaender/studierende-und-praktikanten.html) [official, cantonal; legal basis not cited on the page]
- Federal SEM stagiaire agreements (young professionals): countries are Argentina, Australia, Chile, Indonesia, Japan, Canada, Monaco, New Zealand, Philippines, Russia, San Marino, South Africa, Tunisia, Ukraine and USA — **Mexico is not on the list**; applicants need a completed education (at least a Bachelor's or a two-year vocational apprenticeship), maximum stay 18 months; position must be in the learned field; part-time work not allowed — [SEM Berufspraktikum (DE)](https://www.sem.admin.ch/sem/de/home/themen/arbeit/berufspraktikum.html) [official]; French page lists the same 15 countries — [SEM stagiaires (FR)](https://www.sem.admin.ch/sem/fr/home/themen/auslandaufenthalt/stagiaires.html.html) [official]. Page does not cover students (Studierende), only people with completed training.
- Voluntary (non-mandatory) internships for non-EU/EFTA applicants are "legally possible only in very limited exceptional cases"; internships generally require employer-filed work permit under quota (L permit under 1 year; B permit 1 year or more) — [USI internship permits guide](https://www.usi.ch/en/university/info/career/students/internship/documents-permits-insurance), [ibani Swiss internships guide](https://www.ibani.com/en/guides/student-jobs-internships-switzerland.md) [snippet/secondary]
- Swiss "Inlaendervorrang" (Art. 21 AIG): non-EU workers can be hired only if no Swiss/EU/EFTA candidate is available, in principle also for paid internships; Zurich parliament document names Art. 30(1)(g) AIG as basis for mandatory internships — [Zurich parliament doc](https://parlzhcdws.cmicloud.ch/parlzh5/cdws/Files/601e7d17e1884728b4cb8bdd2e0b2016-332/1/pdf) [snippet; legal text not read]
- IAESTE Switzerland (the Swiss committee) places Swiss students abroad; incoming placements run through the reciprocal IAESTE network — [IAESTE Switzerland](https://iaeste.org/member-countries/switzerland) [snippet]
- IAESTE Mexico exists: national office at Instituto Tecnologico de Durango (Coordinacion de Lenguas Extranjeras); open to professional-level university students from any Mexican state; 80+ countries; contact via WhatsApp 618 100 8756 (article dated 30 May 2026); no UTM affiliation or Swiss placement data found — [Posta Durango, 30 May 2026](https://www.posta.com.mx/durango/asi-puedes-estudiar-por-intercambio-en-el-extranjero-si-eres-de-durango/vl2210064) [aggregator/news]
- Fellowship-specific permits: ETH puts visa responsibility on the fellow with ETH Student Exchange help and pays visa costs ([ETH SSRF FAQ](https://inf.ethz.ch/studies/summer-research-fellowship/faqs.html)); EPFL gives interns student status with simplified visa/residence procedures ([EPFL practical info](https://www.epfl.ch/education/international/en/coming-to-epfl/research-internships/practical-information/)); CERN sends visa invitation letters around May for openlab ([openlab page](https://openlab.cern/cern-openlab-summer-student-programme)) — all [official]
- EU ETIAS authorisation (applies to Switzerland and Sweden for visa-exempt travellers) has no confirmed start date; reporting points to 2027 — [Dutch government ETIAS page](https://government.nl/topics/holidays-and-travels/etias) [snippet]

### Inferences
- A Swiss corporate summer internship is only plausible if UTM can certify the internship as a *required* part of his curriculum and a placement organisation (IAESTE Mexico) or the employer files the permit; this likely applies to the later "prácticas profesionales" stage rather than summer 2027 (unverified, needs UTM confirmation).
- Because Mexico is absent from the stagiaire list and he has no degree yet, the stagiaire route is not available for 2027 or for a post-bachelor stay unless the treaty list changes.
- Mexican citizens are generally visa-exempt for ≤90-day Schengen stays (general knowledge, not retrieved from an official source here), but short-stay entry does not authorise work; the fellowship programmes manage the status for him.

### Gaps
- Exact permit category (visa/permit type, any L-permit or "guest scientist" status) that ETH SSRF fellows from Mexico receive: not stated on retrieved pages.
- No SEM page directly addressing students at foreign universities: SEM English stagiaire page was unavailable; rules above rely on the Zurich cantonal page and secondary guides.
- Whether UTM's curriculum has a mandatory internship and in which semester: not researched.
- Whether IAESTE Mexico places students in Switzerland/Sweden/Ireland and its 2027 call timing: not found.

## 4. Sweden: work-permit route for non-EU interns

### Takeaway
Sweden has two explicit routes: (a) a residence permit for a *paid* traineeship connected to current higher-education studies (does not require the student to be at a Swedish university per the pages read) and (b) a permit for traineeships through an international exchange programme such as IAESTE or AIESEC. Fee SEK 1,500, typical decision time up to 4 months, application from outside Sweden before travelling.

### Cited Findings
- Traineeship within higher education: must be paid and connected to current studies/degree; applicant needs current enrolment in a degree programme (or a degree no more than two years old); written traineeship agreement in the same field and at the same qualification level; proof of funds and return travel; comprehensive health insurance for stays under one year; pay at least Swedish collective-agreement or customary trainee level (no fixed amount); fee SEK 1,500; 75% of recent cases decided within 4 months; apply from outside Sweden and wait for decision — [Migrationsverket: higher education traineeships, important to know](https://www.migrationsverket.se/en/you-want-to-apply/work/temporary-work-in-sweden/traineeships-internships/traineeships-internships-within-the-context-of-higher-education/flikar/important-to-know.html) [official]
- Employer view: employer starts the application, trainee completes their part; agreement must state goals, supervision, salary, insurance, working hours; maximum 18 months; unpaid traineeships need a visitor's permit instead; page does not say whether the trainee must be enrolled at a foreign university — [Migrationsverket: employer page](https://www.migrationsverket.se/en/employers/you-want-to-employ/employing-someone-from-outside-the-eu-eea/temporary-employment/trainee-intern-within-the-context-of-higher-education.html) [official]
- International-exchange route (AIESEC, IAESTE, JUF, SACCUSA or country-to-country agreement): applicant at least 18, written paid offer, up to 18 months, SEK 1,500, 75% decided within 4 months; if the traineeship is related to education, the higher-education permit applies instead — [Migrationsverket: exchange traineeships](https://www.migrationsverket.se/en/you-want-to-apply/work/temporary-work-in-sweden/traineeships-internships/traineeships-internships-conducted-via-an-international-exchange-programme/flikar/important-to-know.html) [official]
- Employer-side exchange page: no specific salary figure for this permit; a general work-permit salary threshold of SEK 34,470/month (90% of median) is mentioned in a news item but not stated to apply to trainees — [Migrationsverket: exchange employer page](https://www.migrationsverket.se/en/employers/you-want-to-employ/employing-someone-from-outside-the-eu-eea/temporary-employment/trainee-intern-through-international-exchange.html) [official]
- IAESTE Sweden advises applicants to start the permit process as soon as possible; trainees cannot extend beyond their original permit — [IAESTE Sweden](https://iaeste.org/member-countries/sweden) [snippet]

### Inferences
- For summer 2027 the practical route is the higher-education traineeship permit: it needs a *paid* offer, so any company willing to sign a trainee agreement mapped to his degree (computer engineering) and sponsor the application (employer starts it) can bring him; unpaid placements are not an option.

## 6. Ireland: permit routes (Internship Employment Permit, Exchange Agreement Permit, Working Holiday)

### Takeaway
Ireland has a purpose-built **Internship Employment Permit** for non-EEA students enrolled abroad, but it is restricted to Critical-Skills-type occupations, needs a paid job offer, must be filed at least 12 weeks before the start, and costs EUR 500-1,000 (employer-borne). There is **no Working Holiday Authorisation for Mexico**.

### Cited Findings
- **Internship Employment Permit**: foreign nationals who are full-time students (including postgraduate) enrolled at a third-level institution outside Ireland, pursuing a degree or higher, with an internship offer from an Irish employer; the internship must relate to an occupation on the Critical Skills Occupations List and the course of study must be wholly or substantially concerned with those skills shortages, with experience in those skills required to complete the course; maximum 12 months, non-renewable; pay at least National Minimum Wage; fee EUR 500 (≤6 months) or EUR 1,000 (6-12 months), refundable 90% if refused, and the employer cannot recover it from the employee; apply at least 12 weeks before the start date via Employment Permits Online; no Labour Market Needs Test; at application, at least 50% of the employer's employees must be EEA nationals; holder must register with GNIB and the permit is not a residence permission — [Internship Employment Permit (DETE)](https://enterprise.gov.ie/en/what-we-do/workplace-and-skills/employment-permits/permit-types/internship-employment-permit/) [official]
- Critical Skills list scope: the official eligible-occupations page says the list covers areas including ICT; specific codes (SOC 2137 programmers/software development professionals, 2139 web design/development, 221 other ICT professionals) appear in a search summary but not on the fetched page text — [DETE eligible occupations](https://enterprise.gov.ie/en/what-we-do/workplace-and-skills/employment-permits/employment-permit-eligibility/highly-skilled-eligible-occupations-list/) [official page, codes via snippet]
- **Exchange Agreement Employment Permit** (IAESTE/AIESEC): no processing fee; IAESTE permits up to 12 months and non-renewable; pay at least National Minimum Wage; needs letter from the exchange organisation; same 12-week lead time and 50% EEA-staff condition; permit issued to the foreign national with copy to the employer — [DETE Exchange Agreement Permit](https://enterprise.gov.ie/en/what-we-do/workplace-and-skills/employment-permits/permit-types/exchange-agreement-employment-permit/) [official]
- **Working Holiday Authorisation**: Ireland has agreements with 11 countries/areas: Andorra, Argentina, Australia, Canada, Chile, Hong Kong, Japan, New Zealand, South Korea, Taiwan, USA. **Mexico is not included**, so no age limit, quota or application window applies to Mexicans — [Ireland.ie Working Holiday Authorisation](https://api.ireland.ie/en/dfa/visas-for-ireland/working-holiday-authorisation/) [official]. Third-party guides agree there is no Mexico-Ireland agreement — [Holafly](https://esim.holafly.com/visas/irish-work-holiday-visas-mexicans/) [aggregator]
- Short-stay visitor entry for Mexicans is visa-free for 90 days, but working is not permitted on visitor entry — [Peninsula Visa Mexico to Ireland](https://www.peninsulavisa.com/visa-requirements/mexico-to-ireland) [aggregator]
- Stamp-2 student routes (casual work) apply to students enrolled in Ireland, not to students enrolled in Mexico — [Education in Ireland: Working in Ireland](https://www.educationinireland.com/en/Living-in-Ireland/While-You-re-in-Ireland/Working-in-Ireland) [snippet]

### Inferences
- Eligibility for the Internship Employment Permit hinges on his computer-engineering course being "wholly or substantially" about CSOL skills; computer engineering/software roles are the most likely to fit, but this should be confirmed with the employer's immigration counsel.
- Planning: for a 1 June 2027 start the permit application must be received by about 9 March 2027 (12 weeks); the employer needs to apply earlier because an offer must exist first.
- IAESTE is cheaper (no fee) but depends on reciprocity and national-committee allocation.

### Gaps
- Does the permit require the *internship* to be part of the course (the page says "completing the course must require gaining experience")? If UTM's plan does not require an internship, eligibility may be challenged; no case law or guidance retrieved.
- Official CSOL document (docx) not read; current salary/threshold figures conflict across third-party guides (EUR 38,000 vs 40,904) and are not needed for this permit.
- Who files the application (employer vs employee) for Internship Employment Permit: not clear on the page text.
- IAESTE Ireland's current operator and 2027 intake: not verified (page says Leargas; may be outdated).
